How scoring works

One — the language

Exact language100
Same branch — Telugu for Tamil60
Same family, other branch — Hindi for Persian30
Different family entirely0
Answered on one listen, no replay×1.25

Difficulty — how much accent survives

Textbook — late learner, classroom, never left home×1.0
Worn — some immersion×1.25
Eroded — early onset plus real exposure×1.5
Near-native — childhood onset, immersed, resident for years×1.8

Built from the speaker's age of English onset, how they learned it, and how long they've lived in an English-speaking country. An early start only counts for much if immersion backed it up — a classroom learner who never left home keeps their accent whatever age they began. It stays hidden until the reveal: telling you up front would give away half the birthplace question.

Two — the birthplace

Exact country50
Right continent, wrong country20
Neither0

Native language is a poor proxy for accent — a French speaker born in Douala and one born in Lyon share a label and nothing else. No difficulty multiplier here; it isn't a listening task.
